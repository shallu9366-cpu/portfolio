Life Journey & Milestones

The Extraordinary Life of a Visionary

From humble beginnings selling newspapers in Rameswaram to leading India's missile program and serving as the 11th President.

1931 • Early Life

Birth in Rameswaram

Born on October 15, 1931, to a humble Tamil Muslim family in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Worked hard as a young boy distributing newspapers to support his family's finances.

1954 • Education

Graduation in Physics & Aerospace

Graduated in physics from St. Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli, and later studied aerospace engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology (MIT).

1969 - 1999 • Scientific Career

ISRO & DRDO Leadership

Joined ISRO as project director of India's first Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-III). Later headed DRDO, orchestrating successful Pokhran-II nuclear tests and missile projects (Agni & Prithvi).

2002 - 2007 • Presidency

The People's President

Served as the 11th President of India. Widely celebrated as the "People's President" for opening the Rashtrapati Bhavan doors to school children and ordinary citizens.

2015 • Legacy

An Eternal Teacher

Passed away doing what he loved most—interacting with students at IIM Shillong on July 27, 2015. His teachings continue to inspire millions globally.
